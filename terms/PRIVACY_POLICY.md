# Privacy Policy — Bubble Studio 3D

_Last updated: 2026-10-17_

This Privacy Policy explains what data **Bubble Studio 3D Manager** (the "App"), operated by **Bubble Studio 3D**, collects, how it is used and how you can control it. It applies together with our [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md).

## 1. Data we access

When you connect a TikTok account through **Login Kit**, and with your consent, the App requests these scopes:

| Scope | Data | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `user.info.basic` | Open ID, display name, avatar | Identify the connected account |
| `user.info.profile` | Username, profile link, bio | Show the account in the dashboard |
| `user.info.stats` | Follower, following, likes and video counts | Marketing metrics |
| `video.list` | List of public videos with title, cover, and view, like, comment and share counts | Per-video performance metrics |

Connecting Instagram works the same way and reads only profile information and post insights of the business's own account.

We do **not** access private messages, contacts, or any data beyond what is listed above.

## 2. How we use the data

Only to display marketing metrics and performance history to the business owner and authorized operators inside the App. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or automated decisions about people.

## 3. Storage and security

- Data is stored in a private database used only by the App.
- OAuth access and refresh tokens are **encrypted** at rest (Fernet) and are never shown or logged.
- Access to the App is restricted to authorized operators.

## 4. Sharing

We do **not** sell, rent or share TikTok or Instagram data with third parties. Data is only exchanged with the platforms' own APIs to provide the features above. We may disclose data if required by law.

## 5. Retention and deletion

- Tokens are kept while the account is connected. Disconnecting the account inside the App deletes the tokens immediately.
- Previously collected metrics may be kept as history; you can request their deletion at any time (see section 7).
- TikTok access tokens expire after 24 hours and are refreshed automatically; refresh tokens expire after 365 days.

## 6. Your choices

You can at any time:

- disconnect the account inside the App;
- revoke the App's access in TikTok (Settings → Security & permissions → Apps and services) or in Instagram;
- request access to or deletion of your data.

## 7. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, open an issue at <https://github.com/Bubble-Studio3D/.github/issues>.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest revision.
